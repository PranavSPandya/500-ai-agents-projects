# Legal & Trademark Notice

**This is not legal advice.** Consult an attorney for your specific situation.

## App name

The app is named **Grand Theft Auto VI Countdown** — a descriptive fan title indicating it counts down to the release of Grand Theft Auto VI.

## Do you need to own GTA to make this?

**No** — but you also **cannot use Rockstar's trademarks freely** just because you don't own them. Trademark law protects the brand owner (Take-Two Interactive / Rockstar Games), not you.

Owning nothing does **not** mean you're automatically safe. Unauthorized **use** of someone else's trademark is what creates risk.

## What Take-Two / Rockstar own

- **Grand Theft Auto** (registered trademark)
- **GTA** (widely protected as part of the franchise)
- Official logos, cover art, trailer footage, character names in commercial contexts
- Game assets and marketing materials

## Why fan apps often still exist

Many fan countdown apps and websites operate because they:

1. Clearly state they are **unofficial** and **not affiliated** with Rockstar
2. Do **not** use official Rockstar logos or re-host copyrighted trailer/screenshot files
3. **Link out** to official sources (YouTube, Rockstar.com) instead of copying content
4. Are **free** and non-commercial (no ads, no paid downloads)
5. Use the game name only to **describe** what the app is about (nominative fair use — a legal defense, not a guarantee)

This app follows those practices: open-source placeholder art, links to official posts, visible disclaimer.

## What increases your risk

| Factor | Risk level |
|--------|------------|
| Free PWA, clear disclaimer, no logos | Lower |
| **App Store listing** named "Grand Theft Auto VI Countdown" | **Higher** — stores and rights holders scan listings |
| **Ads or affiliate revenue** using the GTA name | **Higher** — commercial use of trademark |
| Using official Rockstar logos or trailer images in the app | **High** — copyright + trademark |
| Implying official partnership ("Official GTA VI app") | **Very high** |
| Charging money for the app | **Higher** |

Take-Two has historically enforced its IP against unauthorized projects, mods, and apps that cross these lines.

## App Store reality

Apple and Google may **reject** your app during review for trademark infringement even if Rockstar never contacts you. Using "Grand Theft Auto" in the store title is a common rejection reason.

**Safer store listing options** (if rejected):
- "GTA VI Countdown" (still risky)
- "VI Countdown — Leonida" (lower risk)
- Subtitle: "Unofficial fan countdown for Grand Theft Auto VI"

You can keep the in-app title as **Grand Theft Auto VI Countdown** while using a softer name on the store listing.

## What this app does to reduce risk

- Prominent **unofficial / not affiliated** disclaimer in the footer
- No Rockstar logos or copyrighted official artwork stored in the repo
- Sneak peeks link to **official** social posts (no re-hosting trailers)
- Leonida Preview uses **open-source** synthwave art, not Rockstar assets
- No claim of endorsement, partnership, or official status

## Affiliate links & ads

If you enable **Amazon Associates** or **AdMob**, you are monetizing an app that uses the GTA name commercially. That is **more legally sensitive** than a free fan project. Disclose affiliates (FTC requirement in the US) and accept that Take-Two could object.

## Bottom line

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Will a disclaimer guarantee you're safe? | **No** — it helps but doesn't grant permission |
| Can Rockstar/Take-Two send a cease & desist? | **Yes**, anytime |
| Is a free fan countdown with disclaimers commonly tolerated? | **Often yes** for small personal projects |
| Is publishing "Grand Theft Auto VI Countdown" on app stores risk-free? | **No** |
| Should you talk to a lawyer before monetizing? | **Recommended** |

**You don't need to own GTA to make a fan app — but you do need to accept that using their name without permission always carries some trademark risk, especially on app stores and with ads/affiliates.**
